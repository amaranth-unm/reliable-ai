Choose the AI-based transcription tool based on project needs according to
- First, the language, age, rarity, and style of the handwritten script
- Second, the quality of the reproduction and its layout
- Third, the number of documents you want transcribed
- Fourth, the resources you have in budget, time, and tech comfort

Agentic AI means that you allow an AI application to edit files on your computer
The first popular agentic AI app, called OpenClaw, had many security vulnerabilities
Today, using a frontier model's agentic AI, such as Claude Cowork, is much safer
The October 21st Reliable AI Brown Bag will go into agentic AI in more detail

https://amaranth.unm.edu/reliable-ai/presents/handwritten-transcription/#/example-of-aca-batching

With agentic AI, you can have a LLM do transcription in batches instead of you uploading one-by-one
For document images from the Archive of the Crown of Aragon, this worked with varying levels of success
Agentic AI can output batch HTR into a CSV file, which then can be opened in Excel or easily uploaded as a database on a website


https://amaranth.unm.edu/reliable-ai/presents/handwritten-transcription/#/cautionary-notes

Image recognition drains usage credits quickly
There is a substantial time commitment for perfect transcriptions, known as the verification tax
Consider the use of AI for transcription as more of an axe than a scalpel
Also, be ready for the AI to surprise you with moments of high accuracy
