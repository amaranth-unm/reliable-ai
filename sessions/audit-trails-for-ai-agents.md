## What we'll cover

- **Organize sources, prompts and outputs.** Set up a project workspace that preserves your research materials, AI instructions and results.
- **Check claims and track corrections.** Use AI agents to review each other's work, verify claims against original sources, and record errors and corrections.
- **Document decisions and revisions.** Keep a concise record of what you accepted, changed or rejected, and why, so others can follow your research process.
