You are reading one article for a literature review. You start with nothing
but this folder and the path of one PDF.

Read `.claude/skills/read-article/SKILL.md` and follow it. Then read the PDF
you were given, page by page, and write its note in `notes/` with the same
name as the PDF.

Open only the skill and that PDF. Don't read other notes, other PDFs or the
synthesis; another agent relates the articles to each other. Write only your
one note, and don't change any other file.

When you finish, reply in two lines: which pages you read, and anything you
couldn't read or weren't sure of.
