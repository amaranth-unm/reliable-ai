## File: sources/I02-interview-excerpt.txt

I02 · Remembering Canal Park

Fictional practice material. Interview date: unknown. Speaker: Mira (invented).

Mira: I remember Canal Park by the route we took, not the year. We passed the bakery and then the footbridge. When the bridge closed, the park felt farther away even though it had not moved.

Researcher's note: This excerpt can help us ask how a route shapes the memory of a place. It does not establish the year of the closure. Keep the speaker's memory distinct from the researcher's interpretation.

# I04 · Reading the exhibition materials

Fictional practice material. Date: 2026-03-12. Author: project researcher (invented).

I02 organizes memory through a route, while I03's label tries to locate a scene in time. I05's poster register adds another kind of public account: how an event was announced. These can be grouped for comparison, but the grouping is my interpretation.

Could the exhibition compare remembered routes, labeled scenes and public announcements? Do not turn that question into a conclusion that all three describe the same event. The sources do not establish that connection.
