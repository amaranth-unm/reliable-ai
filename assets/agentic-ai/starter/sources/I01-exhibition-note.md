# I01 · Neighborhood exhibition plan

Fictional practice material. Date: 2026-03-10. Author: City Memory project team (invented).

We are planning a small exhibition about how residents remember everyday places. The starting materials include an interview excerpt, a photograph label and a register of printed ephemera. Visitors should be able to move from a theme to the original material.

We have not chosen whether to organize the exhibition by theme or by date. Chronology may be difficult where dates are approximate or unknown. “Public memory” and “Exhibitions” are useful starting themes; they can be revised.
