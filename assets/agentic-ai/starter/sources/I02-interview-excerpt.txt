I02 · Remembering Canal Park

Fictional practice material. Interview date: unknown. Speaker: Mira (invented).

Mira: I remember Canal Park by the route we took, not the year. We passed the bakery and then the footbridge. When the bridge closed, the park felt farther away even though it had not moved.

Researcher's note: This excerpt can help us ask how a route shapes the memory of a place. It does not establish the year of the closure. Keep the speaker's memory distinct from the researcher's interpretation.
