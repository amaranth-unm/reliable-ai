# Transparency and the black box

Used by: [S01](../notes/S01-hofman.md), [S02](../notes/S02-chiodo.md), [S03](../notes/S03-raley.md)

- **S01 Hofman:** transparency is one of the two "primary labels" (with sustainability). He proposes an A–E openness score from the Foundation Model Transparency Index (pp. 4–5). Transparency here is a property of tools that informs designers' choices.
- **S02 Chiodo:** AI's "inscrutability as a black box" (p. 5, citing Finn and Pasquale) is part of why we cannot imagine it giving reasons for its work. Inscrutability "converges" with Borges's "mysterious" art, but randomness separates them (p. 6).
- **S03 Raley and Rhee:** Ridler's elevation of her data set "gestures toward a prying open of the proverbial black box" (p. 186). "Interpretability and explainability" is their fifth line of inquiry, linked to hermeneutics (pp. 192–193).

**Interpretation (mine):** S01 treats opacity as a tool property to score. S02 treats it as a sign that AI lacks reasons. S03 treats opening it as a task for critical and artistic practice.
