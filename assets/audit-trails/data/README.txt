These are the files attached in the recorded runs of 11 November 2026, and
the files those runs produced. The interest chosen in step 2 was
"historical framing".

ATTACHED IN THE RUNS

WHAT THE RUNS PRODUCED (recorded-trail/)

The files the assistant set out in boxes in its replies, saved exactly as
given. Steps 1 to 4 were one conversation and steps 5 and 6 a second.

  baseline.md        step 1, the whole first overview
  sources.md         step 2, the source register
  notes.md           step 3, one section per source ID
  before-step-6/     step 4, synthesis.md, idea-map.md and reading-queue.md
                     as first written
  checks.md          step 5, the check (15 findings)
  decisions.md       step 6, our decisions and the list of changes
  synthesis.md, idea-map.md, reading-queue.md
                     step 6, revised to follow the decisions
  prompts.md         the run record: R01 to R07 from step 4, then R08 to
                     R11 from step 6, which the assistant gave as new
                     entries and we added to the end by hand

The page asks you to put the assistant's name and the date at the top of
baseline.md. The recording saved the reply as it came, so they are here
instead: Claude Opus 5.5, 11 November 2026.
