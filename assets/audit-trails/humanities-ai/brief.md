# Research brief · prepared example

Question: What do humanities methods contribute to evaluating AI, and what evidence supports those contributions?

Scope: English-language work, 2020–2026. Include research articles and conceptual essays that explain a contribution to AI evaluation. Identify measured effects separately from proposed contributions. Exclude items that only discuss AI as a tool for humanities research.

Stop: up to five candidates, two accessible texts, eight minutes of retrieval. Record access barriers; do not infer full-text content from metadata.

Deliverable: an exploratory synthesis with source locations, checks, decisions and open questions. This is not a systematic review.
