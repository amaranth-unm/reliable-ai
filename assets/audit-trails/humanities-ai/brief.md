# Reading brief · prepared example

Question: How do arts and humanities perspectives help us think about AI?

Interest: creative practice and how it relates to ethical concepts and historical framing. Change this before your live run; it shapes the reading recommendations.

Scope: English-language work, 2020–2026. Include scholarly articles, conceptual essays and dialogues that help explain humanities perspectives on AI. Theory, interpretation and creative practice can all be relevant. Give reasons for selection in relation to the interest.

Stop: up to eight candidates, five accessible sources, eight minutes of retrieval. Keep blocked sources as leads. If fewer than five are ready, record that and use the backup set rather than extending retrieval.

Deliverables: short orientation notes for five sources, an overview, a map of arguments and relationships, and a reasoned reading queue. Check one or two consequential passages during the session; record the remaining coverage as open work.

Version: 3, prepared 2026-10-11. The backup set combines three related essays, a dialogue linked by one essay and a journal introduction. It is an entry point, not a survey of the field.
