PREPARED PRACTICE TEXTS: FICTIONAL LETTERS
Written for the Reliable AI mapping workshop. These are not historical documents.
Dates and movements are invented. Place names refer to real cities.

L1 | Albuquerque, New Mexico | 12 June 1920
Dear Ruth,
I am writing from Albuquerque, New Mexico. Yesterday I returned from Santa Fe, New Mexico, where I spent two days looking at buildings. Tomorrow I hope to go on to Las Vegas, New Mexico, if the arrangements work out. I will write again when I know.

L2 | Gallup, New Mexico | 18 June 1920
Dear Ruth,
I am writing from Gallup, New Mexico. The journey I proposed last week had to wait. Your postcards from Paris, France, are on the table beside me. I have never been to Paris, but the pictures make me want to see it. Tell me what you notice beyond the edges of the photographs.

L3 | Albuquerque, New Mexico | 2 July 1920
Dear Ruth,
I am back in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Your last letter says only Springfield; which state should I put on the envelope? I keep thinking about the buildings in Santa Fe, New Mexico. I may return there next month, but I have made no arrangements.
