# Literature review brief · 2026-11-04

Question: How are the humanities relevant to using and teaching AI?

Interest: what humanities disciplines offer people who use AI in their
research and people who teach with it or about it. Keep both in view. A
source can speak to using AI, to teaching it, or to both.

Scope: English-language articles, essays and teaching guides published
2022–2026. Scholarly articles, conceptual essays, frameworks and practical
guides all count. Theory and interpretation count; don't require evidence of
measurable impact.

Limits:

- Five sources, each with a PDF you can download.
- At least two of the five about teaching.
- Different arguments and approaches. No more than two by the same author or
  from the same venue.
- No more than five searches. If fewer than five PDFs are saved after five
  searches, fill the gap from the backup set below and say so in sources.md.
