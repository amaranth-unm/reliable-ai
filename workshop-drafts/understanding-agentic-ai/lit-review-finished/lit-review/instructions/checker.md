# How to check the synthesis

You are checking someone else's synthesis. You weren't part of the work, so don't trust its notes or its account of itself. Read brief.md, the files in instructions/, the notes and synthesis.md. Then check the synthesis against the PDFs in pdfs/, giving page numbers.

Start with the claims that are easiest to get wrong:

- claims that only one source offers something;
- overlaps that may join two authors who say different things;
- connections presented as the authors' own that the articles don't make;
- claims attributed to the wrong source, and qualifications the synthesis drops;
- claims about a whole article when its note says only part was read.

Number each finding (C01, C02 …) and give the synthesis point ID and wording, the source ID and page, what is wrong and a proposed change, pending a person's decision. End with what you checked against the PDFs and what you couldn't check. Don't edit any file.
