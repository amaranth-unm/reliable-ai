# Literature review brief

Question: How are the humanities relevant to using and teaching AI?

Interest: what humanities disciplines offer people who use AI in their research and people who teach with it or about it. A source can speak to either or both.

Scope: English-language articles, essays and teaching guides published 2022–2026. Theory and interpretation count; don't require evidence of measurable impact.

Limits: four sources, each with a PDF you can download. At least two about teaching. No more than two by the same author or from the same venue. No more than five searches.

Backup set, if the search falls short. Each link is the PDF itself. If a download fails, use the copy in sample-pdfs/ where there is one.

- Lauren M. E. Goodlad, "Editor's Introduction: Humanities in the Loop," Critical AI (2023): https://criticalai.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/CAI112_01Goodlad_1pp-1-1.pdf
