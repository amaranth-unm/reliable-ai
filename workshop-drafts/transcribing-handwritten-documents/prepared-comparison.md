# Prepared comparison for the supervisor demo

Prepared October 13, 2026. This is a teaching illustration. Reading B below changes one word deliberately; it is not a transcript of an AI output. Neither this contrast nor the famous source is a test of an assistant's accuracy on unseen handwriting.

## The original

Abraham Lincoln to Grace Bedell, October 19, 1860, holograph letter. Library of Congress exhibition, “Lincoln's Beard”; lender: Benjamin Shapell Family Manuscript Foundation.

- [Exhibition and item description](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/lincoln/candidate-lincoln.html#obj1)
- [Full image](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/lincoln/vignettes/CandidateLincoln/Assets/al0070_enlarge.jpg)
- [Library of Congress reference excerpt](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/lincoln/ext/transcription20.html)
- Offline image in this repo: _local/lincoln-to-bedell.jpg. It is the downloaded facsimile, with no edits or generated handwriting.

Use the final substantive paragraph, beginning “As to the whiskers”. The Library's excerpt supplies its wording. The line divisions below follow the image; review punctuation against the page if your project requires an exact diplomatic transcript. This reference is a prepared reading, not a claim that a scholar reviewed a new AI run.

## Reading A: prepared reference

```text
L01 As to the whiskers, having never worn
L02 any, do you not think people would
L03 call it a piece of silly affectation
L04 if I were to begin it now?
```

## Reading B: deliberately altered teaching version

```text
L01 As to the whiskers, having never worn
L02 any, do you not think people would
L03 call it a piece of silly affection
L04 if I were to begin it now?
```

Show the image and these two versions. Ask attendees to identify the difference, locate the word on the page and say what would support their choice. “The sentence sounds better” alone does not resolve the handwriting.

| Location | A | B | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| L03, final word | affectation | affection | Enlarge the word; examine the extra letters in the original; compare the published reference after the image check. |

Reveal the reference afterward. Also inspect L04, where both versions agree. The lesson is how to examine a reading and preserve the review decision. If a live run is correct, use a real question about its output instead of pretending it made the constructed error.
